Job: understand "classic pre-renewal, 5x/5x/5x" in one glance, then create an account and download the client. ## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is Prontera's south plaza on a busy evening: the facts float above the stone as merchant vending signboards, and the visitor talks to an NPC to sign up. It refuses the server-site default of a dark epic hero, gold serif, giant character render and a row of rate cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool grey isometric cobble ground (authored SVG tile), the 2002 client's interface language: white windows with a pale periwinkle title bar and hairline navy frame, small rounded grey-blue buttons, vending signboards with a cart glyph, white name tags with a dark outline. DotGothic16 for every in-game voice (signs, tags, window titles, buttons), Nanum Gothic for running text. Accents: NPC-name blue, vending pink, chat green.

STORY: The visitor reads "Ragnarok Forever" as a giant name tag over the plaza, scans the signboards (pre-renewal, 5x/5x/5x), talks to the NPC to create an account or download, then browses the rates window, the six first jobs as a vending shop list, and the guide's three steps in the chat box.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: full-bleed cobble plaza. Top-left a slim client menu window as navigation. Left-centre the huge outlined name-tag title with one plain line under it. Six signboards scattered over the plaza at varied heights, each one real fact. Right: an NPC dialog window, "[Kafra Forever]", with the working register form and the menu option "Descargar cliente". Mobile: name tag, three signboards in a cluster, the NPC window full width.

FORM: Prontera plaza vending, candidate 1 of 7 on the grounded list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK, chosen by the user over the rolled candidate 5); seed key 20b87e53. Signature interaction: signboards bob idly like the client's floating titles; clicking a sign opens its "shop" (scrolls to that section) and the NPC window types its lines out once.
